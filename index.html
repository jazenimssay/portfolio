Chapter I

The Origin Story

this is the whole job, really

I like turning complicated ideas into things people instantly understand.

That has taken me where a wrong click has consequences. A biobanking app, where badly logged data can invalidate research. A crowdfunding platform, where strangers decide whether to trust a startup with their money. Complexity isn’t the problem in either case — unexplained complexity is.
